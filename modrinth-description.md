# Moggilla

**A zombie-apocalypse survival sandbox for Minecraft 1.21.1 (NeoForge), where you outlast the dead, build flying fleets, and carve out a life in a beautifully hostile world.**

The dead never sleep. The world ended in blood moons and shadowed hordes, and the last survivors are those bold enough to take to the skies. Moggilla is a fully-curated **Create-focused apocalypse pack** that fuses brutal undead survival with the freedom of building your own flying machines — every mod, config, resource pack, and shader is pre-configured so you can just hit play.

## 🧟 Survive the Apocalypse

- **Zombie Apocalypse Addon** — the core of the pack. Hour-long escalating difficulty that ramps relentlessly from **Day 3 to Day 50**, and it doesn't pull punches:
  - **Hordes** strike every 5th day at 50% odds with a 3× spawn multiplier
  - **Blood Moons** roll at 15% per night with a 5× spawn multiplier
  - Zombies **break doors and walls** (Day 10), **build to reach you** (Day 15), and **stack into towers** to scale your walls (Day 20)
  - They **don't burn in sunlight** and spawn even during the day — there is no safe corner
- **BleedOut Revive System** — fall to 0 HP and you're downed, bleeding out over 45 seconds. Teammates have 5 seconds to right-click and save you. Teamwork is survival.
- **No Elytras.** Flight is a privilege you build and earn with **Create Aeronautics** airships, **Ornithopter Gliders**, and wind-driven gliders.

## ⚙️ Build & Fly with Create

- **Create 6.0** with mechanical processing, mixing, pressing, and brewing
- **Create Aeronautics** — design, and pilot your own flying ships
- **Create Big Cannons**, **Create: Gunsmithing**, **Sable** airships, **ZipLine**, and more
- Classic combat arsenal via **NukaTeam's Gun Lib** and **Ritchie's Projectile Library**

## 🌍 Explore a Reborn World

- **Terralith + ReStoned** and **Tectonic** — dramatic terrain, underground rivers, lava tunnels, and deep oceans
- **Serene Seasons** — a full season cycle from Early Spring to the brutal mid-winter
- **Distant Horizons**, **Dynamic Trees**, and a huge range of structure mods (**Epic Structures**, **Towns and Towers**, **Philips Ruins**)
- **Alex's Mobs**, **Naturalist**, and **Cosy Critters** — 100+ animals and monsters, each carefully re-tuned

## 🎨 Immersion & Player Experience

- **Iris Shaders** with **Complementary Shaders — Reimagined** bundled
- **Simple Voice Chat** pre-configured for multiplayer
- 8 bundled resource packs (Fresh Animations, Motschen's Better Leaves, and more)
- Full dash of QoL: **JEI**, **AppleSkin**, **Traveler's Backpack**, **Carry On**, **Ping Wheel**, and a dozen more

## 🚀 Performance Out of the Box

Sodium, Lithium, **C2ME**, ModernFix, FerriteCore, and more — all pre-configured for smooth play.
